## Detailed description

ScamShield checks suspicious messages, links, and payment requests for scam
patterns without you leaving the page you're on.

**How it works**
Select any text on any page, right-click, and choose "Check with
ScamShield." A machine-learning model trained on real scam messages, plus a
set of explicit rules for known tricks (shortened links, urgency language,
requests for your PIN or OTP), returns a risk score and explains exactly
why something was flagged.

**Scan a whole page at once**
Click "Scan this page for suspicious links" in the extension popup to flag
risky-looking links right where they appear, entirely inside your browser —
no page content is sent anywhere for this feature.

**Built for UPI and digital payment scams specifically**
Common patterns covered: fake KYC-expiry messages, "collect request" tricks
where accepting a request sends money rather than receiving it, fake
customer-support numbers, and screen-sharing-app social engineering.

**Privacy by design**
- The extension only acts on a page when you tell it to — it does not run on
  every site you visit.
- Only the specific text you select or paste is ever sent for checking.
- No account, no sign-up, no page tracking.
- Full privacy policy: https://mnvv08.github.io/scamshield/privacy-policy.html

**Open source**
The full model, rules, and code are public:
https://github.com/Mnvv08/scamshield
